10 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

How does your doorstep repair service work?+

You book a repair online or via phone. A certified technician arrives at your location and repairs your device on the spot, usually in 30 minutes or less.

What locations do you serve?+

We currently serve Chennai and nearby areas. You can check availability by entering your PIN code during booking.

Are the technicians verified?+

Yes. All our technicians are background-verified, trained, and certified to handle both iOS and Android devices.

What kind of warranty do you offer?+

We offer up to 3 months warranty on parts and service, depending on the type of repair.

Do I need to pay in advance?+

No. You only pay after your device is successfully repaired and you're satisfied with the service.

What if the repair takes longer than expected?+

Most repairs are completed within 30 minutes. If extra time is needed due to complexity, our technician will inform you in advance.

Do you use original parts?+

We use high-quality OEM or Grade-A parts depending on the model and your preference. Warranty covers all parts used.
